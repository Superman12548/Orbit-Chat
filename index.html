Orbit Chat roles

These are Orbit Chat roles inspired by common community-chat roles.

👑 OwnerPermanent, exclusive to ambrosewang@westminster.net. Full control of roles and chat administration.
🛡️ AdminManage member roles and remove group conversations. Cannot change the Owner or other Admins.
🔨 ModeratorCommunity moderation role. Moderation actions beyond the features shown in this version are not yet implemented.
🤝 HelperHelps answer questions and guide members; no extra technical permissions in this version.
💎 VIPA recognition role; no extra technical permissions in this version.
👤 MemberStandard account: find people, join conversations, and send messages.
🔇 MutedCan read accessible conversations but cannot send messages.
